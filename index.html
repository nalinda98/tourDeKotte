🌏 Indo–Lanka Ramayana Trail

A sacred journey on two wheels

The Indo–Lanka Ramayana Trail is a cross-border cultural and spiritual cycling initiative tracing sacred locations associated with the Ramayana across India and Sri Lanka.

Designed as a slow pilgrimage, the trail promotes cultural diplomacy, environmental responsibility, and people-to-people connections.

  • Strengthening India–Sri Lanka cultural ties
  • Promoting non-motorised pilgrimage tourism
  • Supporting local communities and livelihoods
  • Preserving Ramayana-linked heritage landscapes

Sri Lankan Route Highlight:
Kankesanthurai → Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte
Passing temples, villages, forests, and ancient capitals associated with Lanka Pura.
